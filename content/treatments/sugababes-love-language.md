## The idea

Cinema is a projected dream. The lights go down, the room leaves the waking world behind, and this film runs on the same logic.

~~

In a 1920s movie palace at midnight, the Sugababes are the only audience. As the song plays, the auditorium reconfigures: the seating multiplies into infinity, the drapes billow, dust turns in the projector beam, the screen's light ripples over the seats like the bottom of a pool.

## The Story

The form is a fashion film crossed with a music video. The band performs to camera, confident and seated or moving through the room. The room follows dream-logic.

![](assets/treatments/sugababes-love-language/Clip_5.mp4)

{{split
left:
**Beginning**. A violinist plays in the pit. The curtain parts on a white screen and the dream starts there. The screen is the love object and the key light. It pulls at the room from the very first frame.

**Middle**. They take turns performing from different parts of the room while it reconfigures around them. The camera travels the rows to find a performance in a pocket of light.

right:
The whole film runs on the codes of a Golden Age Hollywood premiere: the styling, the lighting (the screen, the paparazzi flash, the projector backlight), the location, the film look (halation, gate weave, light leaks, splices, double exposure). Those codes turn something simple into sophistication.

**End**. They stand and walk away from camera into the white screen and the picture burns out.

}}

## The Location

There are two versions of this film.

~~

1. The small build: a batch of cinema seats, drapes, carpet and a few props, up on any stage or studio in a day. It can be picked up alongside any other shoot and still play as a movie palace. This is the version if we pick it up alongside the Modern Love shoot.

~~

2. Location shoot: a real cinema or screening room, with the backgrounds extended in CG to give it the grandeur of a 1920s movie palace. I would work with the original space and add the missing architecture around it: proscenium, balcony, the details a grand house would have. Small changes to the room, built up in CG, so a few hours in a screening room becomes a night in a picture palace.

~~

## Performance

They are the stars of their own premiere, and they perform like it. Blocking and lighting change, the whole place transforms around them, and they hold centrestage.

One supporting actor plays every other part in the dream. The same actor is the violinist in the pit, the hands on a press camera in the dark, and the usher searching the rows with a torch.

## Cinematography

> We bring in a DOP from the fashion and beauty world, since that is the language of the film.

![](assets/treatments/sugababes-love-language/Clip_16.mp4)
*Previsualisation only. The band performances are shot for real.*

~~

Slow motion and flicker throughout. The band lip syncs to the track playing at double speed and the footage is slowed to match, so the sync is perfect and the movement is slow and dreamlike against the ballad. The flicker comes from the projector itself.

~~

The camera floats. Steadicam drifting the rows, slow pushes onto faces, nothing fast and nothing frantic. Close-ups are held like portraits and wides show the architecture of the room.

~~

Shot digital, finished like 35mm: grain, halation, the print slipping in the gate. The picture behaves like an old print.

## LIGHTING

The lighting is the key, and its changes are the main thing that make this a fashion film. Every light source is motivated. Every light in the film is already in the room: the projector throws the hard backlight, the screen is the giant soft key, the paparazzi cameras give the direct on-camera flash look. That is the whole kit, and it happens to be the same kit a beauty shoot would build: rim, key and flash.
