The idea begins in the corridor. We hold longer than is comfortable, and she
doesn't acknowledge the camera until the second beat. Everything after that
is a chase she doesn't know she's in.

## Opening — the corridor

We open on a slow push. No cut, no music — just the room. The light shifts
from blue to sodium somewhere around the eight second mark and we never
explain why.

![](assets/cargo/grade_ref_still_00019542-copy.jpg)
*Reference — the stillness of the first twenty seconds*

![](wide: assets/cargo/TTRRUUCES-MASTER-060120.mov.01_02_09_17.Still001.jpg)
*Wide frame. The room should feel like it was built for someone else.*

The camera is on a dolly, not a gimbal. That matters — it gives the movement
a mechanical certainty that handheld can't fake. If we can't get a dolly we
shoulder mount it and shoot at 48fps to take the human jitter out.

![](full: assets/cargo/DJANGO_DJANGO_SPIRALS_MASTER_040920_DCP_VERSION_B.00_01_47_14.Still007.jpg)
*Full bleed — this is the frame the whole treatment is built around.*

> She turns at the exact moment the light changes. Not before. The cut is the
> audience catching up.

## Performance

The performance should be close to a person remembering something rather than
performing something. Wardrobe is nearly invisible — dark, matte, no logos, no
texture that catches the light.

**Key notes on direction:**

- We never see her full face until the last thirty seconds
- Movement is always *towards* something, never away
- She doesn't blink on the turn
- The final look to camera is held for four full seconds

### The turn

This is the moment the piece turns from observation into address. It should
feel like the audience has been caught looking.

![](assets/cargo/BCNR_C_MASTER_2K_2.00_04_07_22.Still001.jpeg)
*Reference for the turn — we want the same held tension, but colder.*

## Grade and sound

The grade is desaturated except for the sodium shift in the middle third.
That orange is the only colour in the film and it should feel like an
intrusion.

Sound design carries the second half. No score until the last frame — and even
then, only a single held note that resolves as the image goes to black.
