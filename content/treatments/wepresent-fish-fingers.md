## The director

I am a British director and photographer based in Barcelona. For twelve years I
have directed music videos, commercials and branded films — work that lives or
dies on finding the one image that carries the whole idea.

The move into narrative is not a pivot away from that. It is the same instinct
with more room: performance held long enough to matter, and a camera that
understands why it is where it is.

I direct from stillness. I would rather hold on a face for eight seconds than
cut around an idea. That discipline is what I want to bring to a short with
actual dramatic stakes.
